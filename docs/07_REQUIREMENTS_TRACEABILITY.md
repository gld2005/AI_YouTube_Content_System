## Traceability rule

No acceptance criterion may be marked complete without:

- an implemented feature or approved equivalent;
- a reproducible validation method;
- evidence such as a test, workbook inspection, macro test log, screenshot, or documented manual test;
- confirmation that related data is preserved.
